How a project runs

You should know what to expect before you commit. Every project follows the same five steps.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We talk through your business or idea, your users, goals, constraints and the real problem underneath the request.

  2. 02

    Define

    We agree on scope, deliverables, priorities, timeline and budget in writing before any build begins.

  3. 03

    Design and build

    I design and develop the product and share progress along the way so there are no surprises.

  4. 04

    Test and refine

    Usability, mobile layout, forms and functionality are checked and corrected.

  5. 05

    Launch and improve

    The agreed deliverables go live. Maintenance and future features are discussed if you want them.
