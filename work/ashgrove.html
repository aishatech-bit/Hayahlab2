Ashgrove Realty

A website for an Abuja real estate development and investment company, built to turn visitors into inspection bookings and WhatsApp conversations.

ashgrove6-k2il.vercel.appOpen live site
Project
Client website for Ashgrove Realty Limited, which develops and sells FCTA-approved land and homes across Abuja.
My role
Design and front-end development, working from the client’s logo, flyers, photos and supplied copy.
Tools
HTMLCSSJavaScriptVercel
Status
Live. No performance figures are claimed here because none have been measured.
The opportunity
Property buyers need to trust the company before they enquire. The client wanted a site that positions Ashgrove as a developer and investor, keeps FCTA approval visible, and shows current offers and listings.
The goal
Make it easy to browse properties, understand the payment plan, and reach the team by WhatsApp or by booking an inspection.
What I built
A limited-time offers banner and slider; property listings filtered by location and house type; a planner showing a 40% initial deposit and a monthly figure over 3 or 6 months; service and investment sections; team profiles; a customer review form with optional photo; an FAQ; and an enquiry form with preferred inspection date and time.
Design decisions
White, black and gold from the client’s brand; the client’s own promo flyers presented as offers; plain-language copy supplied by the client, structured so each section answers a buyer’s question.
What I’d improve next
Add the company’s own site photography, and a simple way for staff to update listings and offers without editing code.
Lesson
Building from a client’s existing brand material taught me to agree scope, copy and reference designs in writing before design starts.
